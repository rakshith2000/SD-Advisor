How these are calculated

Action agreement
Verdicts of agreed or agreed with edits, as a share of all verdicts. This is the headline measure: whether the recommendation presented to the lead was the correct course of action. Target {{ target_agreement }}%.
Actionable precision
The same numerator, but excluding already handled from the denominator, so it measures only whether flagging the ticket was justified. Target {{ target_precision }}%. This is the guard against alert fatigue.
Review coverage
Share of recommendations that received any verdict. Low coverage means the other two numbers are drawn from a small, self-selected sample and should be read with caution.
Silent breaches
Tickets that breached SLA without ever being flagged as at risk beforehand. Recall, not precision — the failure mode that a precision-only dashboard hides. This should be zero.
